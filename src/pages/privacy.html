The app

NEXGUARD records notification events (app, time, event type) and, if you choose, notification titles. They are stored only on your phone. The full message text is not stored. The app does not request the internet permission and has no accounts, ads or analytics.

Retention and deletion

You choose how long history is kept and can clear it in the app. Uninstalling removes it.

This website

The website sets no cookies and has no analytics. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device. The site is hosted on Render, which may keep standard server logs. Downloads are served by GitHub.
