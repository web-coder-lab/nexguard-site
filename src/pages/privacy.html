The app

NEXGUARD records notification events (app, time, event type) and, if you choose, notification titles. They are stored only on your phone. The full message text is not stored. The app does not request the internet permission and has no accounts, ads or analytics.

Retention and deletion

You choose how long history is kept and can clear it in the app. Uninstalling removes it.

This website

The website sets no cookies, has no analytics and does not load third-party scripts, fonts or media. Your light/dark theme and optional sound-effects preference are saved in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. The site is hosted on Render, which may keep standard server logs. Downloads and repository pages are served by GitHub when you open those links.
