Find out why your notifications go quiet.

NEXGUARD records when notifications reach your Android phone, checks the settings that block background apps, and shows you the fix for your phone brand. Everything stays on your device.

What NEXGUARD can and cannot do

{{icon:check}} It can

  • Record notification events Android delivers to it
  • Show what changed in your settings
  • Point you to the right settings screen

{{icon:x}} It cannot

  • Stop your phone from closing other apps
  • Restart an app that was force-stopped
  • Recover a notification that never reached your phone
