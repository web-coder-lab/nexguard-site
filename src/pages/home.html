Find out why your notifications go quiet.

NEXGUARD is a free, open-source Android app. It records the notifications Android delivers, checks the settings that block background apps, and shows you the fix for your phone.

{{icon:download}} Download for AndroidTry it in your browser
NEXGUARD {{version}}{{author}}Free, open source, {{size}}

Record what Android actually delivered.

Every notification that is posted or removed, for the apps you choose. Saved on your phone with the app, the time and the event type. No message text.

Check what can be checked.

Notification access, monitoring state and battery policy, as Android reports them. Where Android will not say, NEXGUARD does not guess.

Then fix it, for your phone.

OPPO, Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Huawei, OnePlus and others close background apps. NEXGUARD points you to the exact settings. The OPPO guide matches the developer's own phone.

Phone guides

Private by default.

History is metadata only unless you opt in to titles, and it stays on your device. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send it anywhere.

Security and data practices

Make it yours.

Four themes, reduced motion and haptic feedback. 25 language options, machine-translated and not yet reviewed by native speakers.

Choose your apps.

Switch monitoring per app. New installs start with your own apps on and system apps off.
