ANDROID / NOTIFICATION HEALTH

When notifications go quiet, find out why.

NEXGUARD records the notification events Android delivers, checks background settings and helps you find the next useful step — without pretending it can control the whole phone.

Make the invisible visible.

See which notification events the system delivered to NEXGUARD and when. A small, local timeline helps separate “nothing arrived” from “something changed.”

Review notification access, monitoring state and battery policy as the operating system reports them. Where Android cannot provide an answer, NEXGUARD should not invent one.

06 / APP SELECTION

Choose which apps to watch.

Set monitoring per app instead of treating every package the same. New installs start with user apps enabled and system apps excluded; existing choices are not overwritten.

07 / PRIVACY & LIMITS

Private by design. Honest about the limits.

NEXGUARD does not request the internet permission. It cannot stop Android from closing apps, restart an app that was force-stopped, or recover a notification that never arrived.

A CLEAR BOUNDARY

Know what it can — and cannot — do.

Good troubleshooting starts with clear limits.

{{icon:check}} What NEXGUARD can do

  • Record notification events Android delivers to it.
  • Show monitoring and permission states it can read.
  • Explain relevant background and battery settings.
  • Keep its app history on your device.

{{icon:x}} What NEXGUARD cannot do

  • Stop Android or a manufacturer from closing another app.
  • Restart an app that was force-stopped by the user or system.
  • Recover notifications that never reached the phone.
  • Guarantee that a specific setting will fix every device.
